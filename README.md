# vim-vimrc



I must not touch the mouse.\
The mouse is the flow-killer.\
The arrow key is the little-death that brings total inefficiency.\
I will face Normal Mode.\
I will permit the buffers to pass over me and through my registers.\
And when the macros have gone past, I will turn the inner eye to see their path.\
Where the bloated IDEs and modern forks have gone, there will be nothing.\
Only pure Vim will remain.
